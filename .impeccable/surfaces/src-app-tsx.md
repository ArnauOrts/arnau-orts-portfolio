# Surface brief: portfolio (home)

Scope: the single-page portfolio at `src/App.tsx`. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads from the first viewport).
Audience: recruiters and hiring tech leads first, freelance clients second (see PRODUCT.md). Action: read a project, download the CV, contact.
Proof: projects with what was done in frontend, backend and data. Real content still pending; samples are labeled "Ejemplo / Example", missing details "Pendiente / Pending".
Constraints: ES/EN parity with a switcher; contact and CV always one gesture away; no invented projects, employers, metrics.
History: replaces the "daylight section" world (rejected by the user on 2026-10-01: building metaphor, muted colours, condensed italic type, not modern enough).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Type is the interface. The name and every project are spoken in enormous variable grotesk that widens and thickens under the pointer, set on flat saturated colour fields, one field per project. Refuses the category default of a small name hero over a grid of equal cards, and refuses any illustrative metaphor.

OWN-WORLD: Paper #F3F1EC ground and ink #111111 type. Full-bleed project fields in cobalt #2F3BFF (paper text), vermilion #FF5A36 (ink text) and chartreuse #C6F432 (ink text). Chartreuse is also the single accent for the active or hovered state on paper and ink grounds. Display in Anybody variable (wdth 50 to 150, wght 100 to 900), uppercase, tight leading. Reading text and labels in Geist variable. Square-cornered, flat, no shadows, no gradients, no hairline dividers between sections: sections meet at hard colour cuts.

STORY: The visitor meets the name at billboard scale, plays with it, sees the role and the two actions, and the first project's cobalt field already rising into view. They scroll project by project, each field stating what was built on the interface, the API and the data. They see technologies sized by how much the projects prove them, then experience, then contact on the ink field.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim header (name small left, nav centre, ES/EN and Contact right). The name fills the full content width across two lines at about 11vw, letters reacting to pointer proximity. Below it a row: role and one-line intro at left, the primary action (see projects, ink) and CV at right. The bottom of the viewport is the top edge of the first project's cobalt field, its title already visible.

FORM: kinetic variable typography on colour fields (grounded candidate 5 of 7, assigned by the roll). Seed key 7cc5305b. Raises kept: live pointer response (ASCII render), sequenced numbered layer reveal (neon sign), hard colour cuts between sections (cloud quarry), identical data band per project (brine calendar), one accent for the active state (VU meter). Signature interaction: letter-by-letter width and weight response to the pointer in the name. Motion grammar: type breathes (width and weight), blocks never slide sideways.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
